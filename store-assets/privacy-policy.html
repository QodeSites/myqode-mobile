myQode Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

myQode ("we", "our", or "the app") is a private client portal developed by Qode Invest. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the myQode mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and client code provided at registration.
  • Portfolio data: Investment holdings, NAV history, transaction records, and performance data associated with your Qode Invest account.
  • Authentication tokens: A secure JWT token stored locally on your device to maintain your session.
  • Usage data: App interaction logs for debugging and improving the experience (no third-party analytics).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and provide access to your portfolio data.
  • To display your investment performance, transactions, and documents.
  • To send push notifications about important account updates (with your permission).
  • To improve app performance and fix issues.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Authentication tokens are stored using expo-secure-store, which uses the device's secure enclave (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • We do not store portfolio data locally beyond in-memory caching during your session.
  • No sensitive financial data is stored on your device permanently.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • As required by law or regulatory authority.
  • To Qode Invest's registered servers for delivering your portfolio data.

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following services:

  • Google Fonts (offline): Fonts are bundled within the app — no external font requests.
  • Expo Push Notifications: For sending account alerts. Governed by Expo's Privacy Policy.

7. Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.
  • You may opt out of push notifications at any time via your device settings.
  • You may request a copy of your personal data held by Qode Invest.

8. Children's Privacy

myQode is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
